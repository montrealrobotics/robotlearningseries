The [Robotics and Embodied AI Lab](https://montrealrobotics.ca/) and [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en/) are hosting the Fall 2026 edition of the Mila Robot Learning Seminar; a set of virtual talks by researchers in this field. Seminars usually take place at 11:00 Eastern time on Thursdays and are streamed on [our YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCOouaBg4gHIlNvPkJn_8ooA){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

![]({{ '/assets/img/banner.jpg' | relative_url }})

We have an exciting lineup of speakers for this newest edition of the seminar series! Follow us [on Twitter](https://twitter.com/MontrealRobots){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} for more updates.
