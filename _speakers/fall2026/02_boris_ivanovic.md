abstract: |-
    Everything that moves will be automated. Alpamayo is NVIDIA’s open Physical AI platform striving to make that future possible. I'll begin this talk with an overview of Alpamayo and its "Agentic Factory," an automated development loop that connects ideas to data, models, training, and evaluation. We’ll then take a look inside that loop: how reasoning traces are autolabeled to create useful supervision; how new model architectures explore reasoning representations and world understanding while balancing efficiency and interpretability; how simulation enables closed-loop evaluation and training; and how agents help design experiments and judge results. Finally, I’ll share our latest results on generalizing across autonomous driving and robotics, including how the same development workflow can adapt to different embodiments.
