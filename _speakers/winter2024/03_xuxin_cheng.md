abstract: |-
  Legged robots are capable of traversing challenging terrains thanks to their high degrees of freedom. They are also capable of doing manipulation tasks such as pressing a button with its feet or picking and placing objects with an attached arm. However, these skills necessitate precise coordination between visual perception and whole-body movements. In this talk, I will focus on how to achieve dynamic, scalable skills for legged robots in real-world settings, by tightly combining visual feedback with whole-body movements.
