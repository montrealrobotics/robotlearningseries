# Talk abstract (from the YouTube description)
abstract: |-
  AI agents that can learn decision-making grounded in their own experience will eventually surpass the abilities of agents that learn only from static datasets. Reinforcement learning (RL) is one of the primary mechanisms in AI for creating agents that learn from trial-and-error how to best achieve their goals. In this talk, I will give an overview of my lab’s research in reinforcement learning (RL) and scaling it toward real-world applications. The talk will have three parts. In the first, I will discuss challenges and solutions that arise when integrating RL into complete robotic systems based on my lab’s work using RL to compete in the robot soccer domain. In the second, I will discuss the challenge of building confidence in AI agents before their decisions have real-world consequences. Finally, I will discuss the use of RL to train LLMs how to “reason” and how this approach suggests an exciting new direction for creating agents that simultaneously learn to reason and act.
