abstract: |-
  Long horizon mobile manipulation in real environments requires robots to perceive dynamic scenes, plan over long timescales, and act with reliable dexterity under uncertainty. This talk presents recent methods for open world tasks by combining language conditioned goal specification, object and articulation aware representations, and hierarchical policies that couple task and motion planning with reactive control. Results on long horizon mobile manipulation across homes, labs, and warehouses show reliable performance on tasks such as tidying, assembly, and tool use, along with safe recovery behaviors and human in the loop supervision.
