Organized by [Miguel Saavedra-Ruiz](https://mikes96.github.io/), [Daniel Lawson](https://danielblawson.github.io/), and [Liam Paull](http://liampaull.ca).

The [Robotics and Embodied AI Lab](https://montrealrobotics.ca/) and [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en/) hosted the Fall 2025 edition of the Mila Robot Learning Seminar; a set of virtual talks by researchers in this field. 
We had an exciting lineup of speakers for this edition of the seminar series!
